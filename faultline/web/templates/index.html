    New analysis

    Give Faultline a packet capture. It tells you whether the network is broken or under attack, what to fix first, and which packets prove it.

    Analyze a capture

    Options

    Pseudonyms instead of addresses, so the report is safe to share. Evidence downloads are turned off.

    Sensitivity

    High catches subtler problems but may raise more false alarms.

    The file is analysed on this computer and deleted as soon as the report is ready.

    Is my network broken, or is someone breaking it?

    One of four verdicts, in plain language, with the reasons, the fix and the exact packets behind it.

    • HealthyNothing needs your attention.
    • Minor issuesSmall settings worth tidying up.
    • FaultySomething is broken, like lossy Wi-Fi or a dead DNS server.
    • Under attackSomeone is spoofing, scanning or intercepting traffic.
    1. Drop a captureFrom Wireshark, tcpdump or your router. It never leaves this computer.
    2. 40 checks runARP, DHCP, DNS, TCP, ICMP and TLS behaviour, each weighed fault against attack.
    3. You get a verdictWhat to fix first and the packets that prove it.
